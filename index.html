SURVIVAL RULES

  1. You have 3 LIVES. Every out (strikeout, fly out or ground out) costs one life.

TIPS

  1. Early swing sends the ball left, late swing sends it right. Too far either way is foul.
  2. HIGH goes farther but fielders can catch it. LOW is a grounder.
  3. Single under 200 ft, double 200 ft+, triple 300 ft+, home run over the 375 ft fence.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. The pitcher throws. A bar at the bottom shows where the ball is.
  2. Press SWING (click, tap, SPACE) when the white marker is in the GREEN zone.
  3. Early = ball goes left, late = right. LOW / MID / HIGH sets hit height.
  4. The farther it flies, the more bases you earn. Over the fence = HOME RUN!
  5. Fielders try to catch it. Your runners wait on their bases until you hit.
